4. Cookie Policy — Extended Notice

4.1 What cookies are

Cookies are small text strings that the websites visited by a user send to that user's terminal (computer, tablet, smartphone), where they are stored and then sent back to those same websites on the user's next visit. While browsing, users may also receive on their terminal cookies from websites other than the one they are visiting (so-called "third-party" cookies), set directly by the operators of those websites.

4.2 Types of cookies used on this website

  • Technical and session cookies: strictly necessary for the correct functioning of the website (for example, to save the state of cookie consent through the consent management tool, or to remember display preferences such as the light or dark theme). Pursuant to Article 122 of the Italian Personal Data Protection Code, these do not require the user's prior consent.
  • Analytics and statistical cookies: used to collect information, in aggregate form, on the number of users and on how they visit the website (Google Analytics 4). These are installed only after the user has given explicit consent through the dedicated banner.

4.6 Local storage for offline use

This website can be installed on your device as an application. To allow it to work without a connection, a service worker keeps a copy of the main pages and of the static resources — stylesheets, scripts, fonts and icons — in your browser's storage (Cache Storage).

This is a technical form of storage: the data remains on your device, comes exclusively from this website, contains no information relating to you, is never transmitted to any server and allows no form of profiling. Under Article 122 of the Italian Personal Data Protection Code it therefore does not require your prior consent — but that provision exempts from consent, not from informing you, which is the reason this section exists.

You can remove this copy at any time, in two ways: by clearing your browser's browsing data for this website, or by uninstalling the application from your device's Home screen.

9. Your Rights (Articles 15-22 GDPR)

Users may at any time exercise the rights provided for by Articles 15 to 22 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, including:

  • Right of access (Article 15): to obtain confirmation as to whether processing is taking place and to access your personal data.
  • Right to rectification (Article 16): to obtain the correction of inaccurate data or the completion of incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure / right to be forgotten (Article 17): to obtain the erasure of your data in the cases provided for by law (for example, data no longer necessary, or withdrawal of consent).
  • Right to restriction of processing (Article 18): to obtain the restriction of processing where certain conditions are met (for example, where the accuracy of the data is contested).
  • Right to data portability (Article 20): to receive, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, the data provided to the Data Controller on the basis of consent, where technically applicable.
  • Right to object (Article 21): to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on the legitimate interest of the Data Controller.
  • Right to withdraw consent: to withdraw at any time a consent previously given (for example, for analytics cookies), without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making (Article 22): this website carries out no automated decision-making and no profiling producing legal effects concerning the user.

To exercise any of these rights, users may send a written communication to the following email address: info@giovannipaolofusco.it. Requests are handled free of charge and answered without undue delay and, in any event, at the latest within one month of receipt of the request, as provided for by Article 12 GDPR.
