Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Levels 1, 2 and 3 are for anyone who uses a computer at work, and level 3 requires no code. Level 4 is for whoever runs the company's IT.

Where does it take place?

At your premises, in southern Lazio and the province of Latina.

How many people can attend?

Up to 8 per session. From 9 to 12 with a €150 supplement. Beyond 12, a second session.

Does the AI Act require training?

Article 4 of the AI Act, in the text in force since 27 July 2026, asks those who use AI systems to take measures to support their staff's AI literacy. It sets no specific level and no specific penalty. A documented course is the simplest measure to take.

Is company data safe?

The exercises never use real customer data. Part of the course covers what never to upload and how to set up the tools.

Can it be funded through training funds?

Not directly through me. If you work with a training provider, I can teach the course as a trainer within their project.

Do you issue a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of attendance, with no legal value.
